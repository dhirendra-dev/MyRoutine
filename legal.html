2. Privacy Policy

Information that may be stored

  • Routine information: routine names, institution/course labels, subjects, periods, timetable assignments and preferences you enter.
  • Task information: task titles, due dates, repeat settings, priorities and completion status.
  • Focus and activity information: focus-session timestamps and durations, task-completion events and derived consistency or analytics summaries.
  • App preferences: settings such as display preferences, focus goals and notification choices.
  • Technical information: your browser may process standard request, hosting, cache and security information when loading the site. The hosting provider may independently log technical data under its own policy.

How information is used

  • To display and operate the features you request.
  • To calculate timetable summaries, task views, focus totals and analytics.
  • To save your preferences and support offline access where available.
  • To protect the service, diagnose errors and improve reliability.

Legal basis and consent

Where applicable law requires consent or another lawful basis, processing should occur only on that basis. Browser permissions, such as notifications, are requested by the browser and can be declined. This application should not request notification permission without a clear user action.

Sensitive information

Do not enter passwords, financial account details, government identity numbers, medical records or other highly sensitive personal information into routine or task fields. The app is not designed to collect or protect such information.

3. Data storage and processing

Browser-local recordsCore routine, task, focus and activity records are stored using browser storage on the device running the app.
No assumed account syncDo not assume data syncs to an account, cloud database or another device. Current features may work without an account.
BackupsIf you export a backup, the exported file is under your control. Store it securely and avoid sharing it publicly.
Offline cacheThe service worker may cache app files for offline use. This cache is separate from your personal routine data.

Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access locally stored app information. Private browsing, browser cleanup, device reset or storage eviction may remove data. Clearing site data can permanently erase records that have not been backed up.

4. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The app may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage, Cache Storage and service-worker caches to remember app data, settings and application files. These technologies are used for functionality and offline support, not to create advertising profiles in the current implementation.

Browser settings let you inspect or clear site data. Clearing it may sign you out of browser-level permissions and delete local routines, tasks, focus history and preferences. The app may also load a third-party icon library from a content delivery network; that provider may receive technical request information when the library is loaded.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect the application and its code. However, no website, device, browser storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and browser updated, use a device lock, avoid shared/public devices for private information and keep backups in a secure location.

Because core records are stored in your browser, the security of your device and browser profile is especially important. Report suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorised access through the contact channel listed below.

7. Retention, access and deletion

Browser-local information generally remains on the device until you delete it, clear the browser/site data, uninstall or reset the relevant browser profile, or the browser removes it. You can use in-app deletion or backup controls where available. Export information before clearing storage if you want to keep a copy.

At present, there may be no central user account or server-side record through which the project maintainer can retrieve or erase your browser-local data. If a future version introduces accounts, cloud sync or server-side records, this policy must be updated to explain access, retention and deletion procedures for those records.
